The Clever Tenali Rama

📖 Story: Tenali Rama and the Thieves

Once upon a time, two thieves planned to steal money from Tenali Ramakrishna's house. Tenali noticed their suspicious activities and decided to teach them a lesson.

He spoke loudly to his wife, saying, "We should put our valuable jewels and money in a box and throw it into the well for safety."

The thieves heard this conversation and waited until night. They thought the box in the well contained valuable treasures.

The thieves spent the entire night drawing water from the well to retrieve the box. Meanwhile, Tenali had cleverly placed the box in a way that made their work difficult.

By morning, the thieves were exhausted. Tenali came out and thanked them for watering his garden all night! Realizing they had been tricked, the thieves ran away.

Moral of the Story:
Intelligence and presence of mind can solve difficult problems. Think wisely before taking action!

👑 Tenali Rama and the Proud Scholar

A proud scholar once claimed that nobody could defeat him in an argument. Tenali Rama calmly asked him to explain a difficult question in simple words.

The scholar struggled to explain his ideas clearly. Tenali showed everyone that true knowledge is not about showing off, but about making others understand.

Moral: True wisdom is shown through understanding, not pride.
