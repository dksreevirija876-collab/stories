The Clever Birbal

Once upon a time, Emperor Akbar ruled a great kingdom in India. He had a wise and intelligent minister named Birbal. Akbar enjoyed asking Birbal difficult questions to test his wisdom.

One day, Akbar asked Birbal, "Birbal, can you tell me how many crows live in our kingdom?"

Birbal confidently replied, "Your Majesty, there are exactly 25,000 crows in our kingdom."

Akbar was surprised and asked, "What if there are more crows than that?"

Birbal smiled and said, "Then some crows must have come from neighboring kingdoms to visit their relatives!"

Akbar asked again, "And what if there are fewer crows?"

Birbal replied, "Then some crows must have gone to visit their relatives in other kingdoms!"

The Ending

Akbar laughed at Birbal's clever answer and praised his intelligence. Once again, Birbal had impressed the emperor with his quick thinking!

What Is the Moral of the Story?

Moral: Intelligence, confidence, and quick thinking can help us solve difficult questions.
